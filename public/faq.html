Pricing & Payments

How is pricing structured?

We work off a different pricing model than most builders. We like to call it the GCW, The Gils Custom Way:

  • Build Fee (Labor)
  • Parts (at cost)
  • Optional Warranty Upgrade
Do you offer payment plans?

We do offer payment plans, with both credit and non-credit options.

Credit-based payment options: We can list your project on gilscustoms.com, which allows you to use PayPal Credit or PayPal Pay in 4.

Non-credit payment option: Venmo or PayPal is the preferred option for non-credit-based payment plans due to their ease of use and quick processing times.

Once your project is quoted, we'll agree on a payment schedule that works for you (weekly or monthly). Payments are then sent directly via Venmo or PayPal.

To begin the build, a minimum of 50% of the build fee and 100% of the material cost must be paid. This ensures parts can be ordered and the project can move forward without delays.

All payments are fully refundable up until parts are ordered. Once materials have been purchased, refunds are no longer available.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Venmo and PayPal for all projects, which are our preferred payment methods due to their ease of use and fast processing times. These options allow us to access funds quickly, helping us order parts sooner and keep your build moving efficiently.

If needed, we can also set up a custom listing on our website, which allows you to check out using PayPal features like Pay in 4 or PayPal Credit, or with a debit or credit card.

Are payments refundable?

Yes, payments are fully refundable up until parts are ordered.

Once your project reaches the point where materials have been purchased, refunds are no longer available. This is because parts are often custom-sourced or specific to your build.

If you have any questions or want to make changes before parts are ordered, just reach out. I'm always happy to work with you to make sure everything is right before moving forward.

More complex or fully custom projects may take longer, especially if there are design revisions or specialty parts involved. I'll always provide an estimated timeline upfront and keep you updated throughout the process.

Yes, keeping you updated is a priority throughout the entire process.

If you ever want an update in between, feel free to reach out. I'm always happy to share where your build is at.

Install & Compatibility

Are these plug and play?

It depends on the type of build.

For RGB setups, these are typically not plug-and-play. The controller needs a dedicated power source, and additional signals (such as turn, running light, or brake/tail) must be wired into the system so the lighting functions properly.

For retrofits or OEM-style lighting (white, red, or amber LEDs without RGB), builds are almost always plug-and-play, with the only requirement typically being a simple power connection for the LED sequencer (if doing animated blinkers or power on/off animations).

Every project is a little different, and I'll always let you know exactly what's required before moving forward. Instructions and a walkthrough video are also provided to make installation as straightforward as possible.

Do you provide installation instructions?

Yes, all builds come with clear installation instructions to help guide you through the process.

I also include a step-by-step walkthrough video showing how everything connects and how to use any included features or controllers. The goal is to make installation as straightforward as possible, even for those with limited wiring experience.

If you have any questions during install, you can always reach out. I'm happy to help.

Can you install these for me?

At this time, I typically do not offer in-person installation, as most of my clients are not local.

All builds are designed with remote customers in mind, and I provide detailed instructions and a walkthrough video to make installation as straightforward as possible.

If you're not comfortable installing them yourself, I recommend having a local shop handle the install. I'm also happy to answer any questions they may have to ensure everything goes smoothly.

Product & Performance

Are your lights street legal?

Street legality can vary depending on your state and how the lights are used.

In general, most vehicle codes allow:

  • Front lighting: white or amber
  • Rear lighting: red, amber, and white

When RGB lighting is set within these colors and used appropriately (running lights, turn signals, brake lights, etc.), it typically stays within normal road-use guidelines. However, using other colors (such as blue, green, purple, etc.) on public roads may not be legal and could result in a ticket.

For OEM-style builds and projector retrofits, I use high-quality components that are designed to meet DOT-style output and performance. That said, some states, especially those with strict inspections like California, may still fail a vehicle simply because the lights are not factory-installed.

For that reason, if you live in a state with annual inspections, it's recommended to keep a set of stock headlights or taillights that can be swapped back on if needed.

Ultimately, it is the owner's responsibility to ensure compliance with local laws and regulations.

What controllers do you use?

I use a range of proven, high-quality controllers depending on the needs of the project. Common options include trusted systems like BlueGhozt and Lighting Trendz Flow Series, which offer advanced features such as sequencing, app control, and full RGB customization.

I also offer a custom in-house controller, designed specifically for my builds. This allows for a cleaner install, better integration, and more flexibility depending on the application.

The exact controller used will depend on your goals, budget, and the features you're looking for. I'll always recommend the best option for your specific setup.

Can I control the lights with my phone?

It depends on the type of build.

For RGB setups, yes. These are typically app-controlled, allowing you to change colors, patterns, brightness, and effects directly from your phone.

For builds that use LED sequencers (non-RGB setups), the lighting behavior is pre-programmed on a computer. These are designed to run automatically and are not controlled through a phone app.

I'll always go over control options with you beforehand so you know exactly how your setup will function.

Warranty & Support

What warranty do you offer?

All builds come with a 30-day limited warranty covering craftsmanship and manufacturing defects.

For those looking for additional coverage, I also offer an optional paid 1-year full warranty. This warranty costs 50% of the build fee plus 100% of the parts cost, in addition to the original quoted price. This extended coverage includes both parts and workmanship for added peace of mind.

Funds collected for the extended warranty are held in a separate account and are not used unless a warranty claim is made. If no claim is needed, those funds remain untouched through the duration of the coverage period.

Please note that shipping costs are not included in the warranty and are the responsibility of the client.

Warranties do not cover damage caused by improper installation, misuse, or external factors. Donor housings (if provided by the customer) are also not covered unless sourced by me.

If you ever run into an issue, just reach out. I stand behind my work and will always do my best to make it right.

Misc

Do you do rush orders?

Yes, rush orders may be available depending on current workload and parts availability.

Because rushing a build requires adjusting the schedule and prioritizing your project, a rush fee may apply. This helps cover expedited parts sourcing and the additional time commitment.

If you're working with a specific deadline, let me know upfront and I'll do my best to accommodate it.

Can I make changes after the build starts?

Changes may be possible, but it depends on how far along the build is.

If parts have not yet been ordered, changes are usually easy to accommodate. Once parts are ordered or the build is underway, changes may be limited and could result in additional costs or delays, especially if new parts need to be sourced.

I always recommend finalizing all details before the build begins, but if something comes up, feel free to reach out and I'll do my best to work with you.

What happens if I supply my own parts?

I'm open to customers supplying certain parts, but there are some guidelines to ensure quality and reliability.

  • Housings: Customer-supplied housings are accepted. I strongly prefer OEM housings, as they are typically better sealed (butyl) and more reliable to work with.
  • Projectors: I'm willing to install customer-supplied projectors; however, I do not warranty supplied projectors if they fail, even if you purchase an extended warranty.
  • LEDs & Controllers: I do not use customer-supplied LEDs or controllers unless they are brands I already work with and trust. This is to maintain consistency and performance across builds.

Any supplied parts that are approved will not be covered under warranty, since they were not sourced by me and are not included in the project's parts cost.

If you're considering supplying parts, feel free to check with me first. I'm happy to review them and let you know if they'll work with your build.

Will condensation occur?

A small amount of condensation can be normal in automotive lighting, especially with temperature changes and humidity. Even OEM headlights and taillights are vented and can experience light condensation from time to time.

That said, every build is carefully sealed using proper materials and methods to minimize the chance of moisture getting in.

If you ever notice excessive moisture, water buildup, or persistent fogging, that's not normal. If your build is within the warranty period, or you've purchased an extended warranty, we'll get it taken care of. Outside of warranty coverage, there may be limited options available.
