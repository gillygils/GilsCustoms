How it works

A simple, transparent process

Most of my clients aren't local, so every build is set up for remote customers from day one.

  1. Free consultation

    Send me your vehicle, your idea and any inspiration photos. No idea is too crazy.

  2. Quote & plan

    You get a clear quote and timeline. 50% of the build fee + parts gets things started.

  3. The build

    Most builds take 6–8 weeks. You get progress updates when parts arrive, during assembly and at completion.

  4. Ship & install

    Final photos and a walkthrough video, then it ships with install instructions. I'm here if you have questions.
