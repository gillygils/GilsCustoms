Our Services

Every project is custom. Here's what I can design and build for you.

Custom Headlight & Taillight Builds

Old or new, classic or modern, it doesn't matter. If you're looking for headlights or taillights that stand out from the crowd while providing exceptional light output, I've got you covered. Every build is custom-designed to match your vision, whether you're after a clean factory-inspired appearance or a one-of-a-kind show car. The result is lighting that looks amazing, performs exceptionally, and is built to last.

Services include

  • Projector Retrofits
  • Sequential Turn Signals
  • RGB/RGBW Lighting
  • Startup Animations
  • Custom DRLs
  • Demon Eyes
  • Halo Installations

PCB Design & Engineering

Off-the-shelf electronics don't always fit your project. Whether you need a custom-sized PCB, specialized LED layout, or electronics for a completely new lighting design, Gils Customs can develop a solution built specifically for your application.

Services include

  • Custom LED PCBs
  • Aluminum-core PCBs
  • Custom RGB/RGBW Boards
  • High-Power LED Boards

Complete Lighting System Development

Whether you're modernizing a classic vehicle or creating a brand new lighting system, Gils Customs can help bring your vision to life. From custom headlights and taillights to off-road and specialty lighting, I design complete lighting systems from the ground up, including mechanical design, custom electronics, PCB development, prototyping, and testing. If you can imagine it, I'll help engineer it into reality.

Services include

  • Mechanical Design
  • LED Selection
  • PCB Design
  • 3D Scanning
  • Prototype Development
